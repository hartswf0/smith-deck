HOW IT WORKS

  1. Tap A or B at the top. Pick a table.
  2. Touch a square. It sounds.
  3. Slide CROSSFADE. Left is A, right is B.
  4. Press ● REC. Press it again: trim the take, export WAV.
  5. Press LINK. One table chops, ducks, filters or feeds the other.
  6. Press OPEN beside A or B. That table fills the screen at full size with all of its own controls. ← BOTH returns.
  7. Press ⋯ for mixer, saved pairs, full screen.
